# Institutional Interview

**Interviewee**
Aleksandar Marjanovic
Hedge Fund Manager
Approximate AUM: $800M

---

# Core Observation

This interview revealed that many established hedge funds continue to build trust primarily through operational excellence, reputation, referrals, and long-term relationships rather than formal verification infrastructure.

The current trust model can be summarized as:

Performance
↓

Satisfied Investors
↓

Recommendations
↓

Reputation
↓

Institutional Trust

Rather than actively marketing the fund, investor referrals become the primary mechanism for attracting new capital.

---

# Insight 1 — Reputation Remains the Primary Trust Engine

Aleksandar explained that the fund grows almost entirely through recommendations.

This demonstrates that institutional trust is still largely relationship-driven.

TTL should not attempt to replace reputation.

Instead, TTL should strengthen reputation with independently verifiable evidence.

Future Trust Model:

Performance
↓

Evidence
↓

Verification
↓

Institutional Confidence
↓

Reputation

---

# Insight 2 — Operational Structure Communicates Trust

Rather than focusing only on returns, Aleksandar highlighted several operational characteristics:

- Tier 1 banking relationships
- Tier 1 brokers
- Best available liquidity
- MAM account structure
- Investor-owned trading accounts
- Performance-only compensation

These are operational trust signals.

TTL should eventually support independent verification of these institutional characteristics.

Potential future verification domains include:

- Broker Verification
- Custody Verification
- Liquidity Verification
- Operational Governance
- Account Structure Verification
- Fee Structure Verification

---

# Insight 3 — Incentive Alignment Builds Confidence

The performance-only fee model demonstrates strong alignment between the manager and investors.

TTL could eventually verify incentive structures as part of institutional governance assessments.

Possible future verification metrics include:

- Management Fee Transparency
- Performance Fee Transparency
- Conflict of Interest Assessment
- Incentive Alignment Score

# Overall Conclusion

This interview confirms that institutional trust today is still heavily dependent on reputation and operational credibility.

Trading Truth Layer should position itself as infrastructure that enhances existing reputation through independently verifiable evidence rather than attempting to replace relationship-based trust.
